Privacy policy

Updated 1 November 2026 - for Scraper Tool 5.0

Scraper Tool is a program for your PC with an online account. This page says what we keep about you, why, for how long, and what you can do about it.

What we keep

  • Your account: name, email, your role, and what you add to your profile (phone, date of birth, ID number, bio, time zone, photo, your look). Your password is kept only in protected form by the sign-in service.
  • Your plan and payments: the plan, your weekly and bonus credits, the payments you send (method, amount, the reference you type) and your invoices.
  • Your downloads: which website, when, what kind, and how many products and pictures were saved - to count your credits. The pictures and lists themselves stay on your PC.
  • Watched shops and stars: the shops you add, their products' public names, links, prices and stock, the changes found, and the products you star.
  • Alerts: the email address you give for alerts and, when you connect it, your chat for phone messages. This includes the short "download finished" messages you ask for. Alerts waiting to be sent are kept 30 days.
  • Invites: your invite code, who joined with it and the rewards.
  • The security log: sign-ins, failed sign-ins, changes to accounts, plans, payments, watched shops and alert settings, downloads, exports and catalogues - with the time, the PC's name, the app version and the internet address, so problems and misuse can be traced. Pages opened and buttons pressed (the name of the button only, never what you type) are part of it too.
  • Where you are signed in: each PC's name and last activity, so you can sign out a PC you do not know.
  • Webhooks and API keys you set up: the addresses and a protected form of each key, never the key itself.

What stays on your PC

Downloaded pictures, product lists (products.csv / .xlsx), catalogues, exports, picture-tool copies, presets, your download History and Queue, the gallery's small pictures and - for the Owner - the nightly backups are saved on your own PC only. A "keep me signed in" login is stored encrypted for your user on that PC.

The assistant understands your requests on your PC; what you type is not sent to us.

The browser button

The Scraper Tool button for your browser talks only to the app on your own PC, and only after you paste the pairing code from the app. When you ask it to read a shop, it reads the pages of that shop that you open, keeps the products in your browser until you send them, and hands them to the app on your PC. It does not read other websites, your passwords or your browsing, and sends nothing to us.

Why we keep it

  • to sign you in and keep your account safe;
  • to give you your plan: count credits, confirm payments, make invoices;
  • to check the shops you watch and send the alerts you asked for;
  • to stop misuse and to find out what happened when something goes wrong.

We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.

How long we keep it

  • Your account: while you have an account.
  • The security log: pages opened and buttons pressed for 90 days; everything else for 2 years. Old entries are removed automatically every night.
  • Download counts and credit use: 2 years.
  • Watch changes and price history: 30 days on Standard, 1 year on Pro.
  • Alerts and webhook messages: 30 days. Invoices: as long as the law asks.

When an account is deleted, its watched shops, stars, alerts and credits go with it; invoices and the security log are kept for the times above.

Your choices

  • Change your details, photo, look and alerts in the app (Profile).
  • Sign out other PCs, disconnect phone messages, switch alerts off, remove watched shops and stars at any time.
  • Clear your download History on your PC whenever you like.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or the deletion of your account: osmanarap83@gmail.com.

Safety

Connections are encrypted (https). Each person can read only their own data; admins see more only with the permissions their role gives them, and everything they change is written in the security log, which nobody can edit or delete.

Children

Scraper Tool is a tool for businesses and is not meant for children under 16.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and important changes are announced in the app. Questions about your data: osmanarap83@gmail.com, or message us on WhatsApp from the help on our home page.
