Privacy policy

Updated 1 November 2026 · for Scraper Tool 4.x

Scraper Tool is a Windows program with an online account. This page says what we keep about you, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it. We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.

What we keep

  • Your account: name, email, password (only as a protected hash, by our login provider), your role, and what you add to your profile (phone, date of birth, ID number, bio, time zone, photo, your look).
  • Your plan and payments: the plan, its period, your weekly credits and bonus credits, the payments you send (method, amount, the reference you type) and your invoices.
  • Your downloads: which website, when, what kind, and how many products and pictures were saved - to count your credits. The pictures and lists themselves stay on your PC (see below).
  • Watched shops: the shops you add, their products' names, links, pictures' addresses, prices and stock as the shop shows them publicly, the changes found, and the products you star.
  • Alerts: the email address you give for alerts, and - when you connect it - your Telegram chat number and Telegram name. Alerts waiting to be sent are kept 30 days.
  • Invites: your invite code, who joined with it (not shown to you by name) and the rewards.
  • Security log (audit log): sign-ins, failed sign-ins, changes to accounts, plans and payments, with the time, the PC's name, the app version and the internet address, so problems and misuse can be traced.
  • Where you are signed in: each PC's name, app and last activity, so you can sign out a PC you don't know.
  • Webhooks and API keys you set up: the addresses, a protected form of each key (never the key itself) and the last messages sent (kept 30 days).

What stays on your PC

Downloaded pictures, product lists (products.csv / .xlsx), catalogues, exports, picture-tool copies, presets, the gallery's small pictures and - for the Owner - backups are saved on your own PC only. A "keep me signed in" login is stored encrypted for your Windows user.

Why we keep it

  • to sign you in and keep your account safe;
  • to give you your plan: count credits, confirm payments, make invoices;
  • to check the shops you watch and send the alerts you asked for;
  • to stop misuse (limits on tries and downloads) and to find out what happened when something goes wrong.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase - hosts the accounts database, the login and the files (installers, profile photos).
  • Telegram - only if you connect it: delivers your alerts.
  • Resend - only if email alerts are switched on: delivers alert emails.
  • open.er-api.com - today's exchange rates (no personal data is sent).
  • GitHub - hosts this website.
  • Your own systems - only the webhooks and API keys you set up yourself.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept while you have an account. Watch changes and price history are kept 30 days (Standard) or 1 year (Pro); alerts and webhook messages 30 days; misuse counters 2 days; invoices as long as the law asks for them. When an account is deleted, its watched shops, stars, alerts and credits go with it; invoices and the security log are kept.

Your choices

  • Change your details, photo, look and alerts in the app (Profile).
  • Sign out other PCs (Profile > Security), disconnect Telegram, switch email alerts off, remove watched shops and stars at any time.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or the deletion of your account: osmanarap83@gmail.com.

Safety

Connections are encrypted (https). Each person can only read their own data; admins see more only with the permissions their role gives them, and their changes are written in the security log. Passwords are never stored by us in readable form.

Children

Scraper Tool is a tool for businesses and is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and important changes are announced in the app.

Contact
