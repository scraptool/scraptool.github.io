Shops that block programs? Read them in your own browser.

Some big hypermarkets and marketplaces only open for real visitors. The Scraper Tool button in Chrome reads them as you see them - category by category - and hands everything to the app.

  1. Open the shop in ChromeGo to its home page, as you always do.
  2. Press the Scraper Tool buttonTick the categories you want, or all of them.
  3. It reads every categoryScrolls, presses "Load more", opens each product for barcodes and pictures.
  4. Send to the appPictures, Excel and catalogues - like any other download.

All online shops. Most are read directly through the product data they publish for search engines, and shops that block programs are read in your own Chrome with the browser button.

Yes. The Free plan gives you 200 credits every week without a card, and new accounts can use every Pro feature free for 7 days.

Where are my files kept?

On your own PC. Pictures, Excel files and catalogues never leave it - only your account and plan are on our server.

With EVC Plus or WhatsApp, from the Plans page in the app. Your plan starts as soon as the payment is confirmed.
