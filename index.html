Product pictures from any shop. Competitors' prices, watched for you.

Scraper Tool is a Windows program for shops and resellers. Download a supplier's products with their pictures in minutes, get told when a competitor changes a price, and send your customers a catalogue on WhatsApp.

Download free See the plans

Free plan: 200 credits every week, no card. New accounts can try every Pro feature free for 7 days.

What it does

⬇️

Download any shop

Type a shop's address: pictures in tidy folders, and the code, name, price, stock and description of every product. Shopify, WooCommerce and most shop websites.

👁️

Watch the market

Add competitors' shops. Our server checks them on time - also while your PC is off - and tells you about new products, price drops and stock.

🔔

Alerts on your phone

Star products and get a Telegram message or an email when the price drops below your target, plus a daily summary.

🛒

Straight into your shop

Export to Shopify, WooCommerce or Excel with your own prices: currency, margin and rounding. Products come in as drafts.

📄

Catalogues for WhatsApp

A PDF catalogue or price list with your logo and prices in one click. Copy it and paste it into WhatsApp.

🖼️

Pictures ready to sell

Resize, make square, compress and rename hundreds of pictures at once. Your originals never change.

Your first download in 3 minutes

1
